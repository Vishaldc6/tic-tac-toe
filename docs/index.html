Summary: Shunya Chokadi: Tic-Tac-Toe is built with complete privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. All gameplay is handled locally on your device.

We do not collect any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

The app does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking tools, or behavioral analytics services.

3. Device Permissions

The application requires no sensitive device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or file storage.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is suitable for players of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

5. Security

We value your trust in using our app. Since no personal data is transmitted or stored on external servers, your private information remains completely safe on your device.
